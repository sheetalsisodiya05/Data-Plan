1. Five stages of the data lifecycle

Stage	What happens in the sales pipeline?

1. Source	---Customer orders, payment records, and product information are generated by the application.

2. Ingestion ---Data is collected from application databases and APIs and transferred into the data pipeline.

3. Transformation ---Raw data is cleaned, duplicate records are removed, and total sales and revenue are calculated.

4. Storage	---Processed sales data is stored in a data warehouse for analytical queries.

5. Serving	---The processed data is provided to dashboards and reports for business users.




2. Five undercurrents

.Security            = Protect data.
.Data Management     = Organize and maintain data.
.DataOps             = Monitor and improve data pipelines.
.Orchestration       = Manage the execution order of tasks.
.Software Engineering = Build reliable code.

------D.Batch v/s Streaming

Answers for the three scenarios

.Nightly finance report:	Batch	-Financial reports may be delayed, but usually one hour of delay does not affect immediate business operations.

.Fraud check at checkout:	Streaming	-Fraudulent transactions may be approved before the system detects suspicious activity, resulting in financial losses.

.Trending now widget:	Streaming	-Trending information becomes outdated, resulting in irrelevant recommendations and a poor user experience.
